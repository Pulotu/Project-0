About Maryann Salt

ClearMark was founded by Maryann Salt, a relationship-driven real estate finance professional with experience across commercial lending, business-purpose investor financing, affordable housing finance, credit structuring, and loan execution.

Maryann has worked across DSCR investor loans, bridge financing, construction and rehab scenarios, SBA lending, multifamily and mixed-use projects, small-balance commercial real estate, and affordable housing finance. Her background includes working with borrowers, brokers, developers, CDFIs, banks, credit unions, public agencies, and institutional capital providers.

ClearMark brings that experience into a practical advisory process: understand the deal, identify likely credit constraints, organize the request, and align the opportunity with capital sources that actually fit the transaction.

What ClearMark Helps Solve

Clarity Before Market

Many deals lose momentum because the request is unclear. ClearMark helps define the loan purpose, use of funds, collateral position, income support, exit strategy, and key risks before lender outreach.

Stronger Lender Presentation

ClearMark prepares financing requests so lenders can quickly understand the deal, sponsor story, structure, supporting facts, and execution path.

Better Capital Alignment

ClearMark focuses on targeted lender alignment instead of broad-market blasting, matching each opportunity with lenders whose credit box, appetite, timing, and structure fit the transaction.

Where ClearMark Adds Value

ClearMark is especially useful when a transaction needs more than a quick rate quote. That includes DSCR scenarios with complexity, bridge or transitional financing, construction or rehab projects, small-balance commercial real estate, sponsor recapitalizations, value-add strategies, and referral partner situations where the client needs a focused financing resource.

Some deals are not difficult because they are weak deals. They are difficult because they are not being presented clearly, are being sent to the wrong lenders, or need a more disciplined structure before they reach the market.

Our Standard

ClearMark is grounded in a simple belief: good real estate financing is not just about finding a lender. It is about presenting the right request, to the right capital source, with the right structure and enough discipline to protect the closing path.

The goal is to help serious borrowers and referral partners move with more clarity, credibility, and confidence from financing scenario to execution.
