Approach

1) Feasibility

Rapid feasibility feedback when inputs are complete—fit, constraints, and next steps before capital submission.

2) Structure + Positioning

We develop a positioning narrative and loan structure to ask for—designed for real lender constraints and execution speed.

3) Controlled Lender Alignment

Targeted outreach to best-fit capital sources based on structure, appetite, speed, and geography—without broad-market “shotgunning.”

4) Execute & Close

Milestone tracking, lender feedback loops, and disciplined coordination through closing so timelines stay protected.

Disciplined Execution (Internal)

ClearMark uses a proprietary internal screening workflow—supported by an AI Capital Scout—to standardize intake, validate constraints, and build a high-conviction execution path. This workflow is internal to ClearMark and not a client-facing tool.

Execution Plan + “First Look” package

As part of execution, we prepare a standardized “First Look” package to support efficient lender review and reduce follow-up cycles.

Coverage

We support business-purpose real estate financing nationwide, subject to state-specific lending, licensing, and product constraints.

Timing depends on completeness of inputs. All financing is subject to underwriting and lender approval.
