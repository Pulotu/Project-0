How It Works

1) Feasibility

Rapid initial review of the deal, sponsor profile, collateral, timeline, and likely lender fit.

2) Package

Clear, lender-ready presentation of the loan request, borrower story, structure, risks, and supporting materials.

3) Targeted Lender Alignment

Focused outreach to best-fit capital sources based on structure, appetite, speed, and execution constraints.

4) Execute & Close

Milestone tracking, lender feedback loops, and disciplined coordination through closing.

ClearMark Execution Standard

Structured Intake

We begin with a focused intake process designed to identify loan size, use of proceeds, sponsor profile, collateral, timing, and key execution risks.

Lender-Ready Packaging

We prepare concise, lender-ready materials that clarify the request, supporting facts, execution path, and risk controls.

Controlled Distribution

We avoid broad-market blasting. Each opportunity is aligned with lenders whose credit box, appetite, and timeline fit the transaction.
